Service 05

Procurement Support

Your practical point of contact in China. Managing suppliers from overseas can be time-consuming. GFIRE TECH provides on-the-ground support to help overseas buyers communicate with suppliers, compare quotations, follow samples and coordinate production.

Procurement support includes

  • RFQ & Quotation Comparison — Compare quotations on an apples-to-apples basis.
  • Supplier Communication — Communicate requirements and follow up with Chinese suppliers.
  • Sample Coordination — Coordinate samples and feedback before production.
  • Price Negotiation — Discuss target costs, MOQ and commercial terms with suppliers.
  • Production Follow-up — Track production progress and supplier commitments.
  • Quality Coordination — Coordinate quality checks and resolve production issues.
  • Packaging — Coordinate product and retail packaging requirements.
  • Shipping Coordination — Support shipping arrangements, documentation and delivery coordination.
